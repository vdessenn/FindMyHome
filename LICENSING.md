## What the AGPL asks of you

Use it, study it, change it, share it. Two obligations come with that:

- If you **distribute** a modified version, you ship its source under the AGPL too.
- If you **run** a modified version as a network service, section 13 asks you to offer that
  source to everyone who interacts with it — including over a network. This is the clause that
  separates the AGPL from the GPL, and it is the reason it was chosen here.

Running an unmodified copy for yourself, or for your household, triggers nothing at all. That is
the intended use of this project.

## Commercial licence

The AGPL does not suit everyone. If you want to build on FindMyHome without releasing your own
source — a closed product, a hosted service, an integration inside proprietary software — a
separate commercial licence is available.

Victor Dessenne holds the copyright on the whole of this codebase and can therefore license it
on other terms. Open an issue on the repository to start that conversation.
